At the top left of this terminal is a small tab titled “Purchase Multiplier”. By default, it’s set to 1x, but can be increased or decreased in steps of 1 or 10 to a limit of 64x. The prices on purchasing buttons will update to respect the change.

When clicking a purchasing button, the displayed item will be added to your inventory in the amount shown by the multiplier, and the displayed amount of currency will be removed from your account.
